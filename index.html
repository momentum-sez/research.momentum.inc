Momentum Research

The Research

How institutions operate across jurisdictions: how rules combine, how obligations change, and how claims are priced, cleared, and resolved.

  • 2026 The Research Programme The ideas connecting the papers, from institutional rules to financial claims and dispute resolution.

Core Papers

  • 2026 How Compliance Composes How requirements from several jurisdictions combine, with a record of which rules apply and who decides.
  • 2026 Lex A language for legal rules, exceptions, authority, and changes in law.
  • 2026 Op: Compliance-Carrying Operations Typed instructions for institutional operations, from authorization through payment, interruption, and resumption.
  • 2026 The Claim as Primitive A common language for financial claims: what determines the payoff, when it is evaluated, and what must be paid.
  • 2026 Admissible Obligation Transitions How claims split, combine, and move between programs while preserving what is owed.
  • 2026 Recourse Dispute resolution from the claim and evidence through the award, execution, and recovery record.
  • 2026 Recourse: A Decentralized Network of AI Courts A decentralized network of AI courts that runs dispute resolution and carries outcomes into execution.

Networks, Markets, and Title

  • 2025 Sovereign Economies on a Decentralized Network Sovereign virtual machines for AI agents, polycentric consensus and selective recognition.
  • 2025 The Institutional World Model Continual learning across sovereign virtual machines.
  • 2026 Accession Networks How common accession instruments can establish a network of recognition in place of pairwise negotiation.
  • 2026 Event-Collect BFT Consensus for transactions whose execution depends on external events and evidence.
  • 2026 One-Way Coupling of Prediction Markets to Automated Market Makers How prediction-market signals can adjust automated prices, and how fees and trade limits control manipulation.
  • 2026 Central Counterparty Risk in Automated Markets Clearing resources when liquidation moves prices: margin, cash timing, loss allocation, and settlement-asset feedback.
  • 2026 Parlay Identification of Ising Couplings Recovering the dependence encoded in joint market prices.
  • 2026 Allocated Title A register for identified physical gold, carrying title and obligations through transfer, delivery, shortages, and returns.
